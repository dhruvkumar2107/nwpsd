## Challenge

University of Delhi had a long-standing student exchange agreement with the University of Melbourne, but the program was significantly underutilized. Despite Melbourne being a top-50 global university, only 12 Delhi students had participated over 3 years. The partnership lacked visibility among students, had no structured credit transfer framework, and competing with other international collaborations for limited budget allocation.

## Approach

### Audit & Diagnosis
We conducted a comprehensive review of the existing MoU and identified critical gaps: no faculty champion network, unclear credit equivalency documentation, and marketing materials that hadn't been updated since 2019. Student surveys revealed that 68% of eligible applicants were unaware of the program.

### Strategic Restructuring
- **Faculty Network Activation:** Identified and engaged 14 departmental coordinators across science, humanities, and commerce faculties to champion the exchange
- **Credit Framework Overhaul:** Mapped Melbourne course equivalencies against Delhi's CBCS framework, creating pre-approved credit transfer packages for 23 courses
- **Student Recruitment Campaign:** Designed targeted outreach across 30+ Delhi colleges using social media, alumni testimonials, and Melbourne campus virtual tours
- **Financial Modeling:** Restructured the fellowship structure—shifting from full-scholarship (limited to 5 students) to partial-fellowship + self-funded model (supporting 15 students)

### Compliance & Documentation
Updated the MoU to comply with UGC Academic Collaboration Regulations 2022, including mandatory provisions for credit recognition, student grievance redressal, and insurance requirements.

## Outcome

- **Student participation:** Increased from 12 students over 3 years to 38 students in first year of restructuring
- **Gender diversity:** Female participation rose from 25% to 47% through targeted outreach
- **STEM inclusion:** Added computer science, mathematics, and environmental science stream exchanges (previously limited to humanities)
- **Partnership renewal:** 3-year renewal signed with pathway for joint research initiatives in climate science
- **Credit transfer efficiency:** Reduced credit evaluation time from 8 weeks to 2 weeks through pre-approved equivalency packages

The partnership is now positioned as a flagship exchange program within Delhi University's internationalization portfolio, with plans to extend the model to 4 additional Australian institutions.
