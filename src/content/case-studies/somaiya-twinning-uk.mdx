## Challenge

Somaiya Vidyavihar University, a multidisciplinary institution in Mumbai with 34 constituent colleges, wanted to establish structured twinning programs with UK universities to offer dual-degree pathways. The university had attempted twinning arrangements previously but faced regulatory hurdles—particularly around UGC credit transfer requirements and state university approval processes. Previous discussions with two UK partners had collapsed due to incompatible credit frameworks and unclear regulatory pathways.

## Approach

### Regulatory Pathway Selection
We evaluated multiple regulatory pathways and determined that twinning under UGC Academic Collaboration Regulations 2022 was the optimal route for Somaiya's goals. This pathway provided clearer credit transfer provisions and required fewer regulatory approvals than the newer FHEI framework (which was still being operationalized at the time).

### Partner University Matching
After screening 12 potential UK partners based on academic compatibility, research alignment, and regulatory track record, we facilitated partnership discussions with a Russell Group university offering complementary programs in:
- Computer Science & Engineering
- Business Administration
- Biotechnology
- Media & Communication
- Architecture

### Program Architecture
- **Structure:** 2 years at Somaiya (Mumbai) + 2 years at UK partner
- **Degree Awarded:** Dual degree from both institutions
- **Credit Framework:** Designed using ABC (Academic Bank of Credits) compatibility, with pre-mapped course equivalencies for 48 courses across 5 disciplines
- **Quality Assurance:** Joint Academic Board with annual curriculum review cycles

### Compliance & Filing
Prepared and submitted all required documentation to UGC under the Academic Collaboration Regulations 2022 framework. Coordinated with Maharashtra State Higher Education Council for state-level clearances. Handled AICTE notifications for engineering twinning programs.

### Student Recruitment & Onboarding
Designed recruitment campaign targeting students completing Class 12 and those in first-year programs. Established clear articulation agreements explaining credit transfer processes, fee structures at both institutions, and visa requirements for UK phase.

## Outcome

- **Program launch:** 5 twinning programs operational within 14 months of engagement start
- **First cohort enrollment:** 45 students across 5 disciplines (75% of target capacity)
- **Progression rate:** 92% of first-year Somaiya students successfully progressed to UK partner (41 students)
- **Academic performance:** Average UK phase GPA of 3.4/4.0 for transferred students
- **Visa success rate:** 98% UK student visa approval for Somaiya graduates
- **Regulatory standing:** UGC recognition maintained with zero compliance issues through first review cycle
- **Expansion pipeline:** 3 additional UK partners in discussion for 2025 cohort expansion

The Somaiya-UK twinning program is now a model for how Indian multidisciplinary universities can establish compliant, high-quality international partnerships.
