## What is Accreditation Support?

Accreditation is no longer optional—it determines institutional funding, student enrollment, and regulatory legitimacy. Our Accreditation Support pillar provides end-to-end assistance for NAAC (including the new NAAC 2.0 framework), NBA program accreditation, UGC-DEB recognition for distance education, and international accreditations like AACSB and ABET.

## Who Is This For?

- **Colleges seeking initial NAAC accreditation** (especially those approaching 10-year UGC mandate)
- **Universities pursuing re-accreditation** with improved grading
- **Engineering and management institutions** needing NBA accreditation for Washington Accord recognition
- **Distance education providers** requiring UGC-DEB approval
- **Institutions targeting international accreditations** for global ranking visibility

## Our Process

### Phase 1: Readiness Assessment
We evaluate your institution against all accreditation criteria—using the exact rubrics and scoring methodologies employed by peer review teams. This identifies critical gaps before formal application.

### Phase 2: Documentation & Evidence Building
Accreditation success depends on evidence documentation. We help compile: IQAC reports, learning outcome assessments, research output summaries, infrastructure documentation, student diversity data, and stakeholder feedback surveys.

### Phase 3: Mock Assessment
Our team—comprising former NAAC peer team members and NBA coordinators—conducts a full mock assessment, scoring your institution exactly as the official team would. This provides realistic grading expectations and targeted improvement areas.

### Phase 4: Application & Interview Preparation
We prepare institutional presentations, coordinate mock viva sessions for faculty and students, and ensure the peer review visit logistics are flawless.

## Expected Outcomes

- Clear understanding of current accreditation score potential
- Structured evidence files ready for peer team review
- Faculty and student preparedness for assessment interactions
- Higher probability of achieving target grading (A or A+ NAAC)
