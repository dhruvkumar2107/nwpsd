## What is MoU Compliance & Management?

Memorandums of Understanding form the backbone of institutional collaborations, yet most Indian universities struggle with inconsistent MoU formats, non-compliance with UGC Academic Collaboration Regulations 2022, and poor lifecycle tracking. Our MoU Compliance service transforms this chaos into a structured, auditable process.

## Who Is This For?

- **International Affairs Directors** managing 50+ institutional partnerships
- **Legal and compliance teams** reviewing collaboration agreements
- **Registrar offices** responsible for MoU documentation and renewal tracking
- **Pro-Vice-Chancellors** signing high-value research or twinning agreements
- **State universities** navigating dual regulatory requirements (UGC + State)

## Our Process

### Phase 1: MoU Inventory & Audit
We catalog all existing MoUs, identify expired or non-compliant agreements, and flag partnerships that lack required UGC or AICTE approvals. This creates a single source of truth for institutional partnerships.

### Phase 2: Template Standardization
We develop MoU templates aligned with current UGC regulations—including mandatory clauses for credit transfer, fee structures, dispute resolution, and intellectual property. Templates are customizable for different partnership types (research, twinning, faculty exchange, student mobility).

### Phase 3: Regulatory Filing Support
Many institutions overlook that certain international MoUs require prior UGC approval or post-facto intimation. We ensure every agreement is filed correctly with the appropriate regulatory body.

### Phase 4: Lifecycle Management
We implement tracking systems for MoU renewal dates, performance benchmarks, and compliance deadlines—ensuring no partnership falls through regulatory cracks.

## Expected Outcomes

- 100% regulatory compliance for all active MoUs
- Reduced legal review cycles through standardized templates
- Automated renewal and compliance tracking
- Risk mitigation against UGC show-cause notices for non-compliance
