## What is FHEI & Twinning Program Setup?

The UGC (Establishment and Operation of Foreign Higher Educational Institutions in India) Regulations 2023 opened a new chapter for international education partnerships in India. Combined with the existing twinning program frameworks, institutions now have multiple pathways for collaborative degree programs. Our FHEI & Twinning Setup pillar navigates this complex regulatory landscape to launch compliant, high-quality partnership programs.

## Who Is This For?

- **Indian universities** partnering with foreign institutions for twinning/dual-degree programs
- **Foreign universities** establishing campuses or collaboration centers in India
- **Private institutions** seeking to offer internationally recognized degrees
- **State universities** exploring twinning programs under UGC 2022 collaboration regulations
- **Professional colleges** (engineering, management, law) wanting foreign degree pathways for students

## Our Process

### Phase 1: Regulatory Pathway Analysis
We evaluate your institution's eligibility and recommend the optimal regulatory pathway—whether FHEI recognition under 2023 regulations, twinning agreement under UGC Academic Collaboration Regulations 2022, or state-level partnership frameworks. Each pathway has different requirements, timelines, and compliance obligations.

### Phase 2: Partner University Matching
Based on your academic programs, institutional goals, and regulatory pathway, we identify and facilitate introductions with compatible foreign universities. Our partner network spans universities in the UK, Australia, Canada, Singapore, and select European institutions.

### Phase 3: Program Design & Credit Framework
We design the twinning or dual-degree program structure—including credit equivalencies using the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) framework, curriculum mapping, learning outcome alignment, and quality assurance protocols.

### Phase 4: Regulatory Filing & Approval
Our team prepares and submits all regulatory applications—whether to UGC, AICTE (for professional programs), or state regulatory bodies. We handle documentation, follow-up, and response to any regulatory queries.

### Phase 5: Launch & Operations Support
Post-approval, we provide operational guidance for student recruitment, fee structure design, faculty training for cross-cultural pedagogy, and ongoing compliance reporting.

## Expected Outcomes

- Compliant FHEI or twinning program operational within 12-18 months
- Structured credit transfer framework recognized by both partner institutions
- Student pathways that meet UGC, AICTE, and foreign university requirements
- Scalable partnership model for multiple program disciplines
